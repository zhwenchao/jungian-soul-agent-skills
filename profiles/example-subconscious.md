## Identified Complexes / 识别的情结

### 🔴 Hero Complex / 英雄情结
> *Confidence: Low*
- **Trigger signal / 触发信号**: Cannot say "I can't handle this"; over-commits when help is available
- **Origin / 起源**: Early responsibility expectations from family; praised for being "the capable one"
- **Defense pattern / 防御模式**: Takes on others' burdens to avoid feeling useless; resents it, then feels guilty for resenting
- **Current impact / 当前影响**: Chronic overwork; difficulty delegating; periodic burnout cycles

### 🟡 Caregiver Complex / 照顾者情结
> *Confidence: Medium*
- **Trigger signal / 触发信号**: Feels responsible for others' emotional states in every interaction
- **Origin / 起源**: Primary caretaker role in family of origin during parental conflict
- **Defense pattern / 防御模式**: Monitors room emotional temperature, adjusts self to keep others comfortable
- **Current impact / 当前影响**: Invisible emotional labor; difficulty receiving care from others

## Core Cycle / 核心循环

```
Trigger (demand from others)
  → Overcommit (cannot refuse)
    → Exhaustion (running on fumes)
      → Withdrawal (cancel social, work alone)
        → Guilt (I let people down)
          → Overcompensation (next demand, I'll do even more)
```

## Multi-Generational Themes / 代际主题

| Generation | Pattern | How It Manifests |
|------------|---------|------------------|
| Grandparents | Survival mode, self-sacrifice | Family narrative: "we endured so you don't have to" |
| Parents | Duty-driven, suppressed desire | Modeled working hard but never asking "what do I want" |
| Self | Achievement-anchored identity | Worth tied to output; difficulty resting |

## Belief Structure / 信念结构

| Layer | Belief | Confidence |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Surface | "I work hard because I enjoy achieving" | 🟢 High |
| Middle | "If I stop working hard, I become worthless" | 🟡 Medium |
| Deep | "I am not enough just by existing" | 🟡 Medium |
