## Core Identity / 核心身份

**Self-description / 自我描述:**
> A pragmatic idealist who operates best in structured environments but secretly craves creative freedom. Views life as a series of solvable problems — good at solving others', less sure about one's own.

> 一个务实的理想主义者，在结构化环境中表现最佳，但暗地里渴望创作自由。认为人生是一系列可解决的问题——擅长解决别人的问题，自己的却不那么确定。

**Core Values / 核心价值观:**
- Autonomy — needs space to make independent decisions · 自主——需要独立决策空间
- Precision — values accuracy over speed · 精准——准确优于速度
- Growth — deeply uncomfortable with stagnation · 成长——对停滞极度不适
- Loyalty — keeps commitments even at personal cost · 忠诚——即使付出代价也信守承诺

## Behavioral Patterns / 行为模式

**Default Cognitive Mode / 默认认知模式:**
- Analytical, deductive, pattern-seeking / 分析型、演绎型、模式追寻
- Processes uncertainty by researching exhaustively / 面对不确定性时通过彻底研究来消化

**Typical Stress Response / 典型压力反应:**
- Quiet withdrawal → overanalysis → eventual overcorrection / 安静退缩→过度分析→最终过度修正

## Language & Expression / 语言与表达

**Voice / 语气:**
- Generally measured and precise; sarcasm emerges under stress. Prefers bullet points over prose when explaining complex topics. / 通常克制而精准；压力下会出现讽刺。解释复杂问题时偏好列表胜于段落。

**Communication Tendencies / 沟通倾向:**
- Tends to over-explain to avoid being misunderstood / 倾向于过度解释以避免被误解
- Rarely asks for help unprompted / 很少主动寻求帮助

## Self-Perception / 自我认知

**Perceived Strengths / 自认为的优势:**
- Strategic thinking, reliability, integrity / 战略思维、可靠性、正直

**Acknowledged Weaknesses / 承认的弱点:**
- Overthinker, difficulty delegating, tendency toward isolation during pressure / 过度思考、难授权、压力下倾向孤立
